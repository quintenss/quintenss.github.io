Khoirunnisa Qonita

Artist Bio

Khoirunnisa Qonita is a visual artist based in Cianjur, West java. Her work mainly with medium such as Charcoal, watercolor and installation with printmaking roots in diverse media. She explores topic about daily life in unusual manner with experimental medium, the aims is to tickle down the audience with new narratives. She holds a BFA from Bandung Institute of Technology, majoring in Printmaking.

"My works primarily about daily life and unique things happened in our daily life. the objects are things that going in almost a surrealistic sense in the normality."
